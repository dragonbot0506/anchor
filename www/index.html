Anchor

Train against the mechanism.

Twenty exercises. Each one is built on a published finding about depersonalization and derealization. Tap one, read the study, play the game. The working assumption of this app: the mechanisms that produce DPDR are known well enough that they can be trained against. Some of that is proven, some is a bet.

Ratings are asked only before and after a game, never during. Symptom-checking keeps DPDR going, so the app won't do it for you. And one honest line: CBT with a therapist is the approach with the strongest evidence. This is a training ground, not a replacement.
